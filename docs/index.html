Hash-chain verifier — the audit trail, checkable by anyone

Every decision the live agent makes is appended to a hash-chained log; each record carries the sha256 of the previous one. Paste any log below (or the pre-built sample) and verify it in your browser — the same check as python3 verify.py in the repo.

Why refusal is the P&L engine: at 0.5× take-profit and 3.0× stop-loss the breakeven win rate is exactly 6/7 ≈ 85.7% — the entry has near-zero naive edge; the stack of things this agent refuses to do is the trade. Sizing: 0.75% of equity per ticket, ≤6 slots, ≤9% portfolio heat, net-delta cap ±30 shares-equivalent at $100k (scales with equity).
