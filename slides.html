The economic engine

Variance risk premium, not pattern reading

The motor is the variance risk premium (Carr and Wu, 2009): implied volatility is systematically richer than what gets realised.

Tested and dropped: candlestick patterns. Marshall, Young and Rose (2007) find no value across a majority of DJIA stocks; Park and Irwin find the positive results concentrate in pre-2000 FX and futures.

The four signals that earn their place: IV level and rank · delta as a probability proxy · DTE and theta geometry · the earnings calendar.

The portfolio gate

SPY, QQQ and IWM are one bet, not three

Caps: 2 positions per correlated beta-group, plus a portfolio net-delta cap expressed as a fraction of equity.

The failure we defend against is not a blow-up. It is clustered stops on a single volatility event, followed by the refusal layer locking out re-entry - leaving a negative account with no premium left to grind back inside a one-week window.

A blow-up is loud and obvious. This one is quiet, and it is the one that actually ends short-premium books.

The trap we built against

Judged and developed on accounts 10× apart

The rules permit development on any account and require judging on a fresh $100,000 account. Every risk rule here is a percentage of equity.

A constant calibrated on the development account makes every judged position ten times too large, and the first bad day is unrecoverable.

  • Equity read from /v2/account at decision time - never cached, never defaulted
  • Account number asserted before any order exists
  • A test proves the same signal produces contract counts exactly 10× apart at identical percentage risk

Execution on stale data

The constraint, turned into the differentiator

Free-tier option quotes are 15 minutes stale. No free real-time options feed exists anywhere, because every vendor pays exchange fees.

  • The underlying is live, and implied volatility moves slower than price
  • So every spread is repriced off the live underlying and the delayed surface, using a Black-Scholes engine validated against Hull's canonical example and put-call parity (max error 1.4e-14)
  • A quote diverging from our own mark beyond tolerance is a refusal before an order exists, not a bad fill afterwards
